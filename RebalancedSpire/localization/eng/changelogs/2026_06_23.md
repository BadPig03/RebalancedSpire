[b]The Ironclad[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Tank[/b]:

  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 2 - At the end of your turn, other players gain 8 (12) Block.

  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 0 - Star Cost X - Gain X Plating. Double X if it's 5 (4) or more.

[b]The Necrobinder[/b]
- Buffed the card [b]Wisp[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 0 - Gain 1 (2) Energy. Transform a random Soul in your Draw Pile into Wisp (Wisp+). Exhaust.
  - New: Skill - Common - Cost 0 - Gain 1 (2) Energy. Transform a random Soul in your Draw Pile into a copy of this card.  Exhaust.
