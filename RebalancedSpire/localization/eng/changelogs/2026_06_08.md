v0.2.5-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.107.0[/b] and [b]RitsuLib 0.4.13[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Ironclad[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Drum of Battle[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - Innate. Gain 8 (12) Vigor. Exhaust.
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - Innate. Gain 4 (6) Strength this turn. Exhaust.

[b]The Necrobinder[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Reaper Form[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 3 - Whenever your Attacks deal damage, apply that much Doom. (Upgrade grants Retain.)
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 3 - Whenever your Attacks deal damage, apply that much Doom. At the end of your turn, kill enemies with at least as much Doom as HP. (Upgrade grants Retain.)

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where Seal of Gold would desync again in multiplayer.
- Fixed an issue where the Withering Presence status effect lost its texture.
- Fixed an issue where the amount of HP healed by Kindred Devotee in multiplayer did not scale with player count.
- Fixed an issue where Wither would not return to the discard pile when Transformed.
