v0.2.3-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.106.1[/b] and [b]RitsuLib 0.4.6[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Silent[/b]
- Adjusted the card [b]Infinite Blades[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Uncommon - Cost 1 - At the start of your turn, add 1 Shiv to your Hand. (Upgrade grants Innate.)
  - New: Power - Uncommon - Cost 1 - At the start of your turn, add 1 Shiv to your Hand. 1 (2) Shivs do not count toward your Hand Size. (Upgrade grants Innate.)
- Adjusted the card [b]Master Planner[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 2 - Whenever you discard a Sly card, draw 1 (2) cards.
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 2 (1) - Whenever you discard a Sly card, draw 1 card.

[b]The Necrobinder[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Wisp[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 0 - Gain 1 Energy. Exhaust. (Upgrade grants Retain.)
  - New: Skill - Common - Cost 0 - Gain 1 (2) Energy. Transform a random Soul in your Draw Pile into Wisp (Wisp+). Exhaust.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where Doormaker's Hunger and Close Look status effects did not correctly apply card Affliction.
- Fixed an issue where Wither would still attempt to damage the player at level 0.
