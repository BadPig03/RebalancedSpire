v0.3.4-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.109.0[/b] and [b]RitsuLib 0.4.58[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Ironclad[/b]
- Reverted the card [b]Expect a Fight[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 (0) - Gain 1 Energy for each Attack in your Hand. You cannot gain additional Energy this turn.
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 2 (1) - Gain 1 Energy for each Attack in your Hand.
- Buffed the card [b]Dominate[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Rare - Cost 1 - Apply 1 (2) Vulnerable. Gain 1 Strength for each Vulnerable on the enemy. Exhaust.
  - New: Skill - Rare - Cost 1 - Apply 1 Vulnerable. Gain 1 Strength for each Vulnerable on the enemy. Exhaust. (Upgrade removes Exhaust.)

[b]The Silent[/b]
- Reverted the card [b]Well-Laid Plans[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 1 (0) - At the end of your turn, you no longer discard your Hand.
  - New: Power - Uncommon - Cost 1 - At the end of your turn, Retain up to 1 (2) cards.
- Reverted the card [b]Accelerant[/b]: rarity increased from Uncommon to Rare.

[b]The Regent[/b]
- Reverted the card [b]Pillar of Creation[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Uncommon - Cost 1 - The first time you create a card each turn, gain 5 (7) Block.
  - New: Power - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Whenever you create a card, gain 3 (4) Block.

[b]Enemies[/b]
- Nerfed the enemy [b]Torch Head Amalgam[/b]: Strong Tackle deals 8 less damage.

[b]Relics[/b]
- Reverted the relic [b]Diamond Diadem[/b]:
  - Old: Ancient - Start combat with 20 Block. Your Block is not removed at the start of your next turn.
  - New: Ancient - Whenever you play 2 or fewer cards in a turn, take half damage from enemies.
- Reverted the relic [b]History Course[/b]:
  - Old: Ancient - At the start of your turn, play a copy of your last played Attack.
  - New: Ancient - At the start of your turn, play a copy of your last played Attack or Skill.
- Removed the relic [b]Neow's Lament[/b].

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where Lava Rock's counter was incorrect.
