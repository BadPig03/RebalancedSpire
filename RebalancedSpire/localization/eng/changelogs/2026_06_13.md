v0.2.7-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.107.0[/b] and [b]RitsuLib 0.4.20[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Regent[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Foregone Conclusion[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - At the end of your turn, choose 1 card from your Draw Pile and put it on top of your Draw Pile. (Upgrade grants Innate)
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 1 (0) - Before your turn starts, choose 1 card from your Draw Pile and put it on top of your Draw Pile.
- Reworked the card [b]Genesis[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 2 - At the start of your turn, gain 2 (3) Stars.
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 2 (1) - At the start of your turn, gain 2 Stars.

[b]The Necrobinder[/b]
- Buffed the card [b]Spur[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Osty heals 8 (10) HP. For every 2 excess HP healed, Summon 1 more.
  - New: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Osty heals 9 HP. For every 2 excess HP healed, Summon 1 more. (Upgrade grants Retain.)
- Nerfed the card [b]Reaper Form[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 3 - Whenever your Attacks deal damage, also apply that much Doom. At the end of your turn, kill enemies whose Doom is greater than or equal to their HP. (Upgrade grants Retain.)
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 3 - Whenever your Attacks deal damage, also apply that much Doom. (Upgraded: At the end of your turn, kill enemies whose Doom is greater than or equal to their HP.)

[b]Enemies[/b]
- Buffed enemy [b]Cubex Construct[/b]: Repeater Blast deals 2 more damage.
- Nerfed the [b]Ruby Raiders[/b] encounter: The combination of Brute Ruby Raider, Assassin Ruby Raider, and Axe Ruby Raider can no longer appear.
- Adjusted elite [b]Phrog Parasite[/b]: Reverted its Max HP, and its Proliferate intent now applies 2 Weak to the player.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where returning the Byrdonis Egg in the Byrdonis encounter did not require targeting an enemy.
