v0.1.4-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.105.1[/b] and [b]BaseLib 3.1.3[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Ironclad[/b]
- Buffed the card [b]Blood Wall[/b]: Block increased from 16 (20) to 18 (22).

[b]The Silent[/b]
- Buffed the card [b]Flick-Flack[/b]: damage increased from 6 (8) to 7 (9).
- Buffed the card [b]Expect a Fight[/b]: cost reduced from 2 (1) to 1 (0).
- Reworked the card [b]Drum of Battle[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Draw 2 cards. When this card is Exhausted, gain 2 (3) Energy.
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - Innate. Gain 8 (12) Vigor. Exhaust.
- Reworked the card [b]Forgotten Ritual[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - If you Exhausted a card this turn, gain 3 (4) Energy. Exhaust.
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - If you Exhausted a card this turn, gain 3 (4) Energy. Increase this card's cost by 1.

[b]The Regent[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Glow[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Gain 1 (2) Star(s). Draw 1 card. Next turn, draw 1 card.
  - New: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Gain 1 (2) Star(s). Draw 2 cards.

[b]Relics[/b]
- Changed the relic [b]Lord's Parasol[/b]: it no longer increases the chance for shops to appear in ? rooms.
- Changed the relic [b]Looming Fruit[/b]: Dragon Fruit is no longer included among the random fruit relics it grants.

[b]Enemies[/b]
- Buffed the Elite [b]Phrog Parasite[/b]: Max HP increased by 6 (9).
- Buffed the enemy [b]Sewer Clam[/b]: Plating increased by 1.
- Buffed the enemy [b]Hunter Killer[/b]: Tenderizing Goop applies 1 more Weak.
- Buffed the enemy [b]Ovicopter[/b]: Max HP increased by 5.
- Buffed the enemy [b]Parafright[/b]: Max HP increased by 3.
- Buffed the enemy [b]Slimed Berserker[/b]: Furious Pummeling deals 1 more damage.
- Nerfed the enemy [b]Corpse Slug[/b]: lower bound of Max HP reduced by 6.
- Nerfed the enemy [b]Sludge Spinner[/b]: Slam deals 1 less damage.
- Nerfed the Elite [b]Infested Prism[/b]: Max HP reduced by 10.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where Seal of Gold would still flash after winning combat without being triggered.
- Fixed an issue where the scaling value of the Rolling Boulder status effect did not match the card description.
