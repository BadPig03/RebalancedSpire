[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Ironclad[/b]
- Reverted the card [b]Expect A Fight[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Rare - Cost 3 - Gain 15 (16) Block. Gains 5 (8) additional Block for each Strength you have.
  - New: Skill - Rare - Cost 2 (1) - Gain 1 Energy for each Attack in your Hand.
- Reverted the card [b]Forgotten Ritual[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Gain 3 (4) Energy. Exhaust.
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - If you Exhausted a card this turn, gain 3 (4) Energy. Increase this card's cost by 1.

[b]The Silent[/b]
- Reverted the card [b]Blade of Ink[/b]: Inky Shivs deal 2 more damage.
- Reverted the card [b]Mirage[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Gain Block equal to Poison on ALL enemies. Exhaust. (Upgrade removes Exhaust.)
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 (0) - Retain. Gain Block equal to Poison on ALL enemies. Exhaust.

- Nerfed the card [b]Salvo[/b]: damage reduced from 12 (16) to 9 (13).

[b]Relics[/b]
- Buffed the relic [b]Beautiful Bracelet[/b]: Swift value increased from 2 to 3.
- Buffed the relic [b]Regalite[/b]: Block value increased from 4 to 6.

[b]Enemies[/b]

  - Hammer Uppercut damage reduced from 14 (18) to 12 (14).
  - The One-Two's base damage reduced from 10 (11) to 9 (10).
- Nerfed enemy [b]Mecha Knight[/b]:
  - Charge damage reduced from 25 (30) to 20 (25).
  - Flamethrower damage reduced from 8 (12) to 8 (10).
- Nerfed enemy [b]Globe Head[/b]: Galvanic stacks reduced from 6 (8) to 3 (3).
