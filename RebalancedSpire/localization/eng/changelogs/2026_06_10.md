v0.2.6-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.107.0[/b] and [b]RitsuLib 0.4.16[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Ironclad[/b]
- Reverted all changes to the card [b]Drum of Battle[/b].

[b]The Regent[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Heirloom Hammer[/b]:
    - Old: Attack - Rare - Cost 2 - Deal 20 (25) damage. Choose a Colorless card in your Hand. Add a copy of that card into your Hand.
    - New: Skill - Rare - Cost 1 - Forge 5. Choose a Colorless card in your Hand. Add a (upgraded) copy of that card into your Hand.
- Reworked the card [b]Foregone Conclusion[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Rare - Cost 1 - Next turn, put 2 (3) cards from your Draw Pile into your Hand.
    - New: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - At the end of your turn, choose 1 card from your Draw Pile and put it on top of your Draw Pile. (Upgrade grants Innate.)

[b]Relics[/b]
- Cards granted by the relic [b]Dusty Tome[/b] now also remain fixed in multiplayer.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where War Hammer did not count enemies that died to Doom.
