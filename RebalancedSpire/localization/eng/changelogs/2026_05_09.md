v0.1.2-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.105.1[/b] and [b]BaseLib 3.1.2[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Defect[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Consuming Shadow[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 2 - Channel 2 (3) Dark Orbs. At the end of your turn, Evoke your leftmost Orb.
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - Dark Orbs you Channel additionally gain 50% (100%) of the damage of the last Dark Orb Evoked.
- Reworked the card [b]Voltaic[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Rare - Cost 3 - Channel Lightning Orbs equal to the number of Lightning Orbs you have Channeled this combat. Exhaust. (Upgrade removes Exhaust.)
  - New: Skill - Rare - Cost 3 (2) - Channel Lightning Orbs equal to the number of Lightning Orbs you have Channeled this combat.
- Reworked the card [b]Refract[/b]:
  - Old: Attack - Uncommon - Cost 3 - Deal 9 (12) damage twice. Channel 2 Glass Orbs.
  - New: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Channel 2 Glass Orbs. Exhaust. (Upgrade removes Exhaust.)
- Reworked the card [b]Glasswork[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Gain 5 (8) Block. Channel 1 Glass Orb.
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Channel 1 Glass Orb. Increase the damage of all Glass Orbs by 2 (3).
- Reworked the card [b]Spinner[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - At the start of your turn, Channel 1 Glass Orb. (Upgrade Channels 1 Glass Orb)
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - At the start of your turn, Channel 1 (2) Glass Orbs and trigger the passive ability of all Glass Orbs.
- Reworked the card [b]Leap[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Gain 9 (12) Block.
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Gain 9 (12) Block. Gain 1 (2) Focus this turn.
- Nerfed the card [b]Synchronize[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - Record new Orbs you Channel. The first time each new Orb type is recorded, gain 1 Focus. (Upgrade grants Innate)
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 2 (1) - Record new Orbs you Channel. The first time each new Orb type is recorded, gain 1 Focus.

[b]Relics[/b]
- Reworked the relic [b]Toasty Mittens[/b]:
  - Old: At the start of your turn, choose 1 playable card in your Draw Pile to Exhaust and gain 1 Strength.
  - New: At the start of your turn, choose 1 playable card from the top 5 cards of your Draw Pile to Exhaust. Gain 1 Strength.

[b]Events[/b]
- Modified the event [b]Reflections[/b]: The Stare option can now copy at most 13 cards.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where entering a Doormaker combat while having Philosopher's Stone could cause the game to softlock.
- Fixed an issue where Booming Conch would trigger the vanilla cost-increase effect.
