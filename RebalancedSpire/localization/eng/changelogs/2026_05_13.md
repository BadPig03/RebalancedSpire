v0.1.3-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.105.1[/b] and [b]BaseLib 3.1.3[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Defect[/b]
- Buffed the card [b]Glasswork[/b]: now grants 5 (8) Block.
- Nerfed the card [b]Spinner[/b]: cost increased from 1 to 2.
- Nerfed the card [b]Consuming Shadow[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - Dark Orbs you channel increase the last Evoked Dark Orb’s damage by an additional 50% (100%).
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - Ethereal. Dark Orbs you channel increase the last Evoked Dark Orb’s damage by half. (Upgrade removes Ethereal.)

[b]The Necrobinder[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Afterlife[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Summon 6 (9). Exhaust.
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 1 - At the start of your turn, Osty heals 4 (5) HP. If Osty is not alive, Summon 3 (4) instead.
- Reworked the card [b]Spur[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Rare - Cost 1 - Retain. Summon 3 (5). Osty heals 5 (7) HP.
  - New: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Osty heals 7 (9) HP. Healing beyond Max HP counts as Summon.
- Buffed the card [b]Pull Aggro[/b]:
  - Summon increased from 4 (5) to 8 (9).
  - Block reduced from 7 (9) to 4 (6).
- Reworked the card [b]Right Hand Hand[/b]:
  - Old: Attack - Uncommon - Cost 0 - Osty deals 4 (6) damage. Whenever you play a card costing 2 or more, return this card from your Discard Pile to your Hand.
  - New: Attack - Uncommon - Cost 2 - Osty deals 11 (14) damage. Whenever you play a card costing 2 or more, put this card into your Hand and reduce its cost by 1.
- Reworked the card [b]Sic 'Em[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Osty deals 5 (6) damage. This turn, whenever Osty attacks this enemy, Summon 2 (3).
  - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Osty deals 5 (7) damage. Whenever Osty attacks this enemy, Summon 2 (3). Exhaust.

[b]Colorless Cards[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Eternal Armor[/b]:
  - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 3 - Gain 9 (12) Plating.
  - New: Power - Rare - Cost 3 - Gain 11 Plating. Plating no longer decreases. (Upgrade grants Retain.)
- Buffed the card [b]Rolling Boulder[/b]:
  - Damage increased from 5 (10) to 10 (15).
  - Damage increase amount increased from 5 to 10.

[b]Relics[/b]
- Reworked the relic [b]Fiddle[/b]:
  - Old: At the start of each turn, draw 2 additional cards. After drawing, if you have more than 8 cards in your hand, automatically discard cards until only 8 cards remain in your hand.
  - New: At the start of each turn, draw 2 additional cards. Your maximum hand size is reduced by 2.

[b]Enemies[/b]
- Buffed the Boss [b]Doormaker[/b]: during the close inspection phase, it reduces the player’s maximum hand size by 4.
- Nerfed the Elite [b]Spectral Knight[/b]: greatly reduced its maximum HP in multiplayer.

[b]Events[/b]
- Changed the event [b]Punch Off[/b]: Punch Constructs’ starting maximum HP increased from 60–80% to 70–80%.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where the screen could turn black when ending the game after defeating Doormaker.
- Fixed an issue where Bone Tea, Ember Tea, and Tea of Discourtesy could appear in the Crystal Sphere event.
