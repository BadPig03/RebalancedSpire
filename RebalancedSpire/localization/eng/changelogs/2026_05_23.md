v0.1.7-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.106.1[/b] and [b]BaseLib 3.1.4[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Silent[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]Scare[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - Apply 1 Weak to ALL enemies. Exhaust. (Upgrade removes Exhaust.)
    - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 2 - Sly. Apply 1 (2) Weak and Vulnerable to ALL enemies.

[b]The Necrobinder[/b]
- Nerfed the card [b]Right Hand Hand[/b]: Damage reduced from 11 (14) to 9 (13).
- Buffed the card [b]Debilitate[/b]:
    - Old: Attack - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Deal 10 (12) damage. For the next 2 (3) turns, Vulnerable and Weak are twice as effective against that enemy.
    - New: Attack - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Deal 10 (12) damage. For the next 3 (4) turns, Vulnerable and Weak are twice as effective against that enemy.

[b]Enemies[/b]
- Reworked Boss [b]Aeonglass[/b]: Now has all-new mechanics and intents.
- Reworked Elite [b]Infested Prism[/b]: Now has all-new mechanics and intents.
- Adjusted Boss [b]The Kin[/b]: The Easter egg reward now grants only 1 additional Common Relic.
- Adjusted Elite [b]Skulking Colony[/b]: Reverted its intents and damage to those from Beta v0.105.1.
- Nerfed enemy [b]Punch Construct[/b]:
    - Adjusted its intent order: Strong Punch now comes before Fast Punch.
    - Now applies Weak instead of Frail.
- Nerfed enemy [b]Haunted Ship[/b]: The number of Dazed cards shuffled into the Discard Pile was reduced from 5 to 3.

[blue][b]Art:[/b][/blue]

- Added final art for the [b]Leap[/b] status effect.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where the description text for Parafright's Illusion status effect was incorrect.
- Fixed an issue where Osty still dealt 100% damage to Fabricator while Fabricator's status effect was active.
- Fixed an issue where the description text for Doormaker's Hunger status effect was incorrect.
- Fixed an issue where stunning Doormaker while it was preparing to flee would cause another Doormaker to appear.
- Fixed an issue where Neow's Lament could not be disabled in the configuration options.
