v0.4.0-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.113.1[/b] and [b]RitsuLib 0.6.14[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]Multiplayer Settings Sync[/b]
- Multiplayer runs now use the host's RebalancedSpire gameplay settings. Guests' personal settings are not overwritten. The active settings are locked once the run begins.

[b]Blitz[/b]
- New routes have 1 additional question-mark room before the Shop in each act, guaranteed to contain an event. Existing routes are unchanged.
  - When the event before the Shop is enabled, the act's event pool is limited to an allowlist. The usual event requirements still apply.
- Adjusted base combat rewards:
  - Weak encounters:
    - Old: Choose 1 of 3 Common cards, with no potion.
    - New: Choose 1 of 3 Common cards and receive 1 random potion.
  - Normal encounters:
    - Old: Choose 1 of 3 Common cards and receive 1 random potion.
    - New: Choose 1 of 3 Uncommon cards, with no potion.
  - Elite encounters:
    - Old: Choose 1 of 3 Uncommon cards and receive 2 random relics.
    - New: Choose 1 of 3 Rare cards and receive 2 random relics.

[b]Cards[/b]
- Reworked the card [b]God Byrd[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Event - Cost 0 - At the start of your next turn, deal 30 (40) damage to ALL enemies.
  - New: Attack - Event - Cost 0 - Deal 25 (32) damage to ALL enemies.
- Added a hidden transformation for [b]God Byrd[/b].
- Buffed the card [b]Mighty Weapon[/b]:
  - Old: Attack - Event - Cost 1 - Deal 25 (30) damage to ALL enemies. Add 2 Dazed to your Draw Pile.
  - New: Attack - Event - Cost 1 - Deal 25 (30) damage to ALL enemies. Add 2 Dazed to your Discard Pile.
- Buffed the card [b]Mighty Shield[/b]:
  - Old: Skill - Event - Cost 1 - Gain 20 (25) Block. Add 3 Dazed to your Draw Pile.
  - New: Skill - Event - Cost 1 - Gain 20 (25) Block. Add 3 Dazed to your Discard Pile.

[b]Relics[/b]
- Reverted the relic [b]Brilliant Scarf[/b]:
  - Old: Ancient - The 6th card you play from your hand each turn is free.
  - New: Ancient - The 5th card you play from your hand each turn is free.
- Reworked the relic [b]Mighty Wings[/b]:
  - Old: Event - Whenever you deal 30 or more attack damage in a single hit, gain 2 Strength.
  - New: Event - Every 4 turns, gain 1 Flight.

[b]Ancient Choices[/b]
- Relaxed the requirements for [b]Pael's Legion[/b]: Pael can now offer it even if you already have an event pet or a Byrdonis Egg in your Deck.

[b]Events[/b]
- Adjusted the Beat Up option in [b]Waterfall of Obvious Secrets[/b]:
  - Old: Obtain a random relic. Add 2 Guilty to your Deck.
  - New: Obtain a random Rare relic. Add 1 Poor Sleep to your Deck.
- Relaxed the appearance conditions for [b]Wuppy in Trouble[/b]: The event can now appear even if a player already has an event pet or a Byrdonis Egg in their Deck.
- Buffed the event [b]Museum of Torture[/b]:
  - Modified the effect of the Take It option:
    - Old: Obtain the displayed Relic. Add a random Curse to your Deck.
    - New: Obtain the displayed Relic. Add 1 Injury to your Deck.
  - Modified the effect of the Break Out option:
    - Old: Lose 10 Max HP.
    - New: Lose 8 HP.
  - Added a lethal-choice warning to Break Out.
- Buffed the event [b]Thermae Nonupeipe[/b]:
  - Reduced the Gold requirement from at least 176 to at least 150 per player.
  - Modified the effect of the Private Oasis option:
    - Old: Lose ALL your Gold. Obtain Bath Robes.
    - New: Pay 150 Gold. Obtain Bath Robes.
  - Modified the effect of the Drink Bath Water option:
    - Old: Lose 31 HP. Add 3 Guilty to your Deck. Obtain one of Nonupeipe's Ancient relics.
    - New: Lose 31 HP. Add 1 Regret to your Deck. Obtain one of Nonupeipe's Ancient relics.

[b]Enemies[/b]
- Adjusted [b]Byrdonis[/b]'s pet interaction:
  - Old: Each player with Byrdpip applies 99 Weak to it at the start of combat.
  - New: Each player with Byrdpip or Byrdine applies 99 Weak to it at the start of combat.

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed missing icons for Bone Tea, Ember Tea, and Tea of Discourtesy.
- Fixed an issue where Tea of Discourtesy could not be thrown at the Merchant using a keyboard or controller.
