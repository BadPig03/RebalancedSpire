v0.2.0-beta Version Update Announcement
[blue]Compatible with [b]Beta v0.106.1[/b] and [b]RitsuLib 0.3.8[/b][/blue]

[blue][b]Content:[/b][/blue]

[b]The Silent[/b]
- Buffed the card [b]Blade of Ink[/b]: Inky Shivs deal 1 more damage.
- Reworked the card [b]Untouchable[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 2 - Sly. Gain 6 (8) Block.
    - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 2 - Sly. Gain 4 (5) Block twice.
- Reworked the card [b]Poisoned Stab[/b]:
    - Old: Attack - Common - Cost 1 - Deal 6 (8) damage. Apply 3 (4) Poison.
    - New: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Add 2 Poisonous Shivs (Shivs+) to your Hand. Exhaust.
- Reworked the card [b]Up My Sleeve[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 2 - Add 3 (4) Shivs to your Hand. This card's cost is reduced by 1.
    - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 2 - Sly. Add 3 (4) Shivs to your Hand.
- Reworked the card [b]Hidden Daggers[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - Discard 2 cards. Add 2 Shivs to your Hand.
    - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 0 - Discard 3 (2) cards. Add 2 Energetic Shivs to your Hand.
- Reworked the card [b]Bouncing Flask[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 2 - Apply 3 Poison to a random enemy 3 times.
    - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 2 - Sly. Apply 2 Poison to a random enemy 3 (4) times.
- Reworked the card [b]Hand Trick[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Gain 7 (10) Block. Add Sly to a Skill in your Hand this turn.
    - New: Skill - Uncommon - Cost 1 - Gain 4 (7) Block. Add Sly to a card in your Hand. Exhaust.
- Reworked the card [b]Master Planner[/b]:
    - Old: Power - Rare - Cost 2 (1) - When you play a Skill, it gains Sly.
    - New: Power - Rare - Cost 2 - Whenever you discard a Sly card, draw 1 (2) cards.
- Reworked the card [b]Shadowmeld[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Rare - Cost 1 (0) - Double your Block gain this turn.
    - New: Skill - Rare - Cost 1 - Retain. Double your Block gain this turn. Exhaust. (Upgrade removes Exhaust.)

[b]The Regent[/b]
- Changed the card [b]The Sealed Throne[/b]: Its upgrade effect was changed from reducing cost by 1 to granting Innate.

[b]The Necrobinder[/b]
- Buffed the card [b]Sic 'Em[/b]: In multiplayer, other players' Osty can now also trigger Summon when attacking that enemy.
- Reworked the card [b]Spur[/b]:
    - Old: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Osty heals 7 (9) HP. Healing above Max HP counts as Summon.
    - New: Skill - Common - Cost 1 - Osty heals 8 (10) HP. For every 2 excess HP healed, Summon 1 more.
- Nerfed the card [b]Debilitate[/b]: Damage reduced from 10 (12) to 7 (10).

[b]Relics[/b]
- Nerfed the relic [b]Neow's Talisman[/b]:
    - Old: Ancient - Upon pickup, Upgrade 2 of your Strikes and 2 of your Defends.
    - New: Ancient - Upon pickup, Upgrade one Strike and one Defend, then randomly Upgrade a card.
- Buffed the relic [b]Blood-Soaked Rose[/b]: Enthralled's cost was reduced from 2 to 1.
- Buffed the relic [b]Choices Paradox[/b]: You now choose from 6 upgraded cards.
- Buffed the relic [b]Sere Talon[/b]:
    - Old: Ancient - Add 1 Writhe and 2 Wishes to your Deck.
    - New: Ancient - Add 1 Guilty and 2 Wishes to your Deck. (Wish: Skill - Ancient - Cost 0 - Innate. Put a card from your Draw Pile into your Hand. Exhaust.)

[b]Events[/b]
- Buffed the event [b]Spiraling Whirlpool[/b]: The relic rarity from the Reach In option is now Rare.

[b]Enemies[/b]
- Nerfed enemy [b]Corpse Slug[/b]: The upper bound of its Max HP variance was reduced by 6.
- Nerfed enemy [b]Skulking Colony[/b]: Max HP reduced by 5.
- Reverted the changes to enemy [b]Shrinker Beetle[/b].

[b]Game-related[/b]
- The dependency library was migrated from [blue][b]BaseLib[/b][/blue] to [blue][b]RitsuLib[/b][/blue].

[blue][b]Bug Fixes:[/b][/blue]

- Fixed an issue where the card art for the Bound Affliction was incorrect.
- Fixed an issue where War Hammer only randomly upgraded 1 card when multiple enemies were killed at the same time.
- Fixed an issue where playing high-cost cards could cause the Withering Presence status effect counter to drop below 0.
- Fixed an issue where Lava Rock still dropped card rewards after the Act 3 Boss combat.
- Fixed an issue where Test Subject's animation did not match after gaining the Enrage status effect.
- Fixed an issue where Chomper's Block only increased at Ascension 9.
- Fixed an issue where Wither did not retain its level after being Transformed.
- Fixed an issue where the amount of HP healed by the Leeching Hug status effect did not scale with player count.
- Fixed an issue where Skulking Colony lost its animations during Crush and Inertia.
- Fixed an issue where Skulking Colony's Block only increased at Ascension 9.
- Fixed an issue where The Insatiable's incoming damage multiplier was calculated incorrectly.
